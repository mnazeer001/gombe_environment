About the Ministry

Our mandate, vision, leadership and the agencies that deliver environmental services across Gombe State.

Our mandate

Established to protect people and the natural environment

The Ministry of Environment & Forest Resources is the principal institution of the Gombe State Government charged with formulating, coordinating and implementing environmental policy. Our work spans regulation and enforcement, natural-resource management, climate action, and the delivery of public environmental services.

Gombe lies within Nigeria's semi-arid belt and faces desertification, soil erosion, declining vegetation cover, flooding and water stress. Reversing these trends — while creating green jobs — is the heart of our mandate.

  • Advise Government on environmental policy, law and standards
  • Regulate activities that pollute or degrade land, air and water
  • Manage forest reserves, shelterbelts and the state tree estate
  • Coordinate climate change adaptation and mitigation
  • Supervise sanitation, waste management and pollution abatement
  • Mobilise communities and partners for environmental stewardship

Our Vision

A clean, green and climate-resilient Gombe State where every citizen enjoys a healthy environment and shares in the benefits of well-managed natural resources.

Our Mission

To protect and restore the environment through effective regulation, evidence-based planning, large-scale land restoration and inclusive partnerships that create sustainable livelihoods.

Core Values

Integrity · Accountability · Professionalism · Inclusiveness · Innovation · Service to the citizen.

Our journey

Milestones

1996

Gombe State created

Environmental functions are carried out under a combined ministry serving the new state.

2011

Erosion & watershed programme begins

The state joins the Nigeria Erosion and Watershed Management Project (NEWMAP) pipeline to tackle advancing gullies in Gombe metropolis and rural catchments.

2019

Gombe Goes Green (3G) launched

A flagship afforestation campaign with a target of one million trees a year, backed by nurseries in every zone and school-based green clubs.

2022

ACReSAL comes on stream

The World Bank-supported Agro-Climatic Resilience in Semi-Arid Landscapes project begins catchment restoration, erosion control and climate-smart livelihood investments.

2024

Waste-to-wealth facility commissioned

A legacy dumpsite is converted into a materials recovery and recycling hub, cutting pollution and creating jobs.

2026

Over 1,000 hectares restored

Cumulative land restoration passes 1,000 hectares with nearly 50km of gullies stabilised across three major erosion sites.

Institutions

Agencies, parastatals & project units

Gombe State Environmental Protection Agency (GOSEPA)

Operational agency for sanitation, waste collection and enforcement of environmental sanitation regulations in the metropolis and major towns.

ACReSAL State Project Implementation Unit

Implements the World Bank-supported land-restoration and climate-resilience portfolio, including catchment plans, erosion works and community grants.

Gombe Goes Green (3G) Secretariat

Coordinates the statewide tree-planting campaign, nursery production, urban greening and green enterprise support.

State-level liaison for the national Great Green Wall programme to halt desert encroachment through shelterbelts and dryland livelihoods.

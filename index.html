Who we are

The custodian of Gombe State's environment and forest estate

The Ministry of Environment & Forest Resources is the arm of the Gombe State Government responsible for environmental policy, pollution control, forestry, climate change adaptation, erosion and flood management, and the conservation of natural resources.

Working with communities, development partners and the private sector, we translate state and national environmental policy into measurable action on the ground — from shelterbelts in the north of the state to gully rehabilitation and waste-to-wealth facilities in Gombe metropolis.

  • Policy, regulation, monitoring and enforcement
  • Afforestation, shelterbelts and community forestry
  • Erosion, flood and watershed management
  • Sanitation, waste management and circular economy

What we do

Our core focus areas

Eight mandates guide the Ministry's work across the eleven local government areas of Gombe State.

Forestry & Afforestation

Management of forest reserves, shelterbelts, roadside plantations and state nurseries, plus the regulation of tree felling and fuelwood harvesting.

Learn more

Pollution Control

Air, water, noise and soil quality monitoring; industrial effluent standards; enforcement of environmental regulations and abatement notices.

Learn more

Climate Change

State climate action planning, greenhouse-gas inventory, adaptation and resilience programmes, and access to climate finance.

Learn more

Erosion & Flood Control

Gully rehabilitation, watershed management, drainage interventions and early warning for flood-prone communities.

Learn more

Waste & Sanitation

Solid waste collection standards, transfer stations, recycling, waste-to-wealth enterprises and monthly environmental sanitation.

Learn more

Conservation & Biodiversity

Protection of wildlife, wetlands and rocky outcrop habitats, and the control of bush burning and illegal wildlife trade.

Learn more

EIA & Compliance

Screening, review and approval of environmental impact assessments and audits for projects sited in Gombe State.

Learn more

Community Mobilisation

Environmental education, school green clubs, women and youth engagement, and support to community-based natural resource management.

Learn more

Data & Research

State of the Environment reporting, GIS mapping of land degradation, and the Gombe Environmental Data Portal.

Learn more

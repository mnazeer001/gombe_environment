Process

How to apply — five simple steps

1. Enquire

Visit the Service Desk or call the Ministry to confirm the category of approval your project requires.

2. Submit

File your application letter with the prescribed documents and evidence of payment of the statutory fee.

3. Review

A technical committee reviews the submission; for EIAs a site verification and stakeholder display may apply.

4. Decision

The Ministry issues an approval, a conditional approval with mitigation requirements, or a refusal with reasons.

5. Monitor

Approved projects are monitored for compliance; audits may be required annually depending on the risk class.

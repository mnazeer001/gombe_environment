Departments & Units

Eight departments deliver the Ministry's mandate, each headed by a Director reporting to the Permanent Secretary.

Directorate of Forestry & forest Resources

Forest conservation, silviculture, timber licensing, log control, agro-forestry, and development of forest reserves.

Departmental responsibilities

Select a department to see its schedule of duties and the services it provides to the public.

Management of the state forest estate, nurseries, shelterbelts and community forestry.

  • Establishment and maintenance of forest reserves and shelterbelts
  • Production and distribution of tree seedlings
  • Issuance of tree-felling and fuelwood movement permits
  • Forest law enforcement and anti-desertification works

Regulation of pollution sources and protection of public health from environmental hazards.

  • Air, water, noise and soil quality monitoring
  • Industrial effluent and emissions compliance inspections
  • Abatement notices, sanctions and remediation orders
  • Management of hazardous and medical waste streams

State climate action, resilience planning and clean-energy transition support.

  • Implementation of the State Climate Change Action Plan
  • Greenhouse gas inventory and climate risk assessment
  • Community adaptation and early-warning initiatives
  • Promotion of clean cookstoves, solar and other renewables

Protection of wildlife, wetlands and fragile ecosystems across the state.

  • Protection of wildlife and their habitats
  • Control of bush burning and illegal wildlife trade
  • Wetland and watershed conservation
  • Eco-tourism development at natural sites

Air, water, noise and soil quality monitoring.

  • Industrial effluent and emissions compliance inspections
  • Abatement notices, sanctions and remediation orders
  • Management of hazardous and medical waste streams

Coordination of solid waste services, public sanitation and the circular economy.

  • Supervision of waste collection and transfer operations
  • Licensing of private waste operators
  • Monthly environmental sanitation exercises
  • Recycling, composting and waste-to-wealth enterprise support

Human resources, records, welfare, training, logistics and general administration of the Ministry.

  • Staff recruitment, postings, promotion and discipline
  • Registry, records and correspondence
  • Office logistics, transport and facility management
  • Staff capacity building and in-service training

Evidence, monitoring and the Ministry's data systems, including the State of the Environment report.

  • Sector planning and project appraisal
  • Monitoring & evaluation of programmes and projects
  • Environmental data collection, GIS mapping and analysis
  • Research partnerships with tertiary institutions
